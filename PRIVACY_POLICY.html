Privacy Policy for ToDo App

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Sankranthi Vinod ("we", "our", or "us") built the ToDo application (Package Name: com.sankranthivinod.todo) as a free application. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

All data entered into the ToDo application—including task titles, descriptions, and completion statuses—is stored exclusively on your device using a local, offline Android Room Database.

  • No Data Sent to External Servers: Your tasks remain 100% private to your device. We do not operate cloud servers that collect or sync your task information.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account or log in to use the app.

2. Permissions Requested

The ToDo app requests standard Android permissions solely for local device functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used for general system framework connectivity checks. No user data is transmitted.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used solely to display local task notifications and reminders on your device if enabled.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

The ToDo app does not use any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics trackers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
