# Privacy Policy for ToDo App

**Effective Date:** November 2, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 2, 2026

**Sankranthi Vinod** ("we", "our", or "us") built the **ToDo** application (Package Name: `com.sankranthivinod.todo`) as a free application. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our Service.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).**

All data entered into the ToDo application—including task titles, descriptions, and completion statuses—is stored exclusively on your device using a local, offline database (Android Room Database). 

* **No Data Sent to External Servers:** Your tasks remain 100% private to your device. We do not operate cloud servers that collect or sync your task information.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the ToDo app.

---

## 2. Permissions Requested

The ToDo app requests the following standard Android permissions to ensure proper app functionality:

* **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Used strictly for general network connectivity checks if required by system framework components. No user data is sent over the network.
* **Post Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used solely to display local task notifications and reminders on your device if enabled.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

The ToDo app does **not** use any third-party software development kits (SDKs), advertising networks, or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) that track user behavior or share data with third parties.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

## 5. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, which is why your data is kept entirely local to your device. The security of your data depends on your device's security settings.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
